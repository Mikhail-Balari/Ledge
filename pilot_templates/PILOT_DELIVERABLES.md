# Pilot Deliverables

A scoped AI Decision Boundary Pilot should produce:

- Decision boundary map.
- Synthetic or approved fixture set.
- Policy definition.
- Executable Ledge demo.
- Unsafe-path check showing unchecked AI use is blocked or explicitly unsafe.
- Audit sample.
- Calibration sample if outcome data is available.
- Calibration limitation note if outcome data is not available.
- Final report.
- Limitations.
- Recommended next steps.

The deliverables are evidence for review. They are not proof of production
readiness or legal compliance.
