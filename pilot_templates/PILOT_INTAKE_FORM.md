# Pilot Intake Form

Use this form to scope one AI Decision Boundary Pilot.

## Workflow

- Where do you use or want to use AI?
- What decision does the AI influence?
- What action can follow?
- Who owns that action today?
- Is the workflow already live, planned, or experimental?

## Risk And Review

- What happens if the AI is wrong?
- Is there human review today?
- What happens below threshold today?
- Is there an escalation path?
- Are there deterministic rules that must always apply?

## Confidence And Outcomes

- Is there a confidence, score, probability, or risk signal?
- How is that signal produced?
- Is there an outcome later that can be compared with the AI output?
- How long does outcome feedback take?
- Is calibration already measured?

## Data Constraints

- What data cannot be shared?
- Can synthetic data be used?
- Can redacted historical examples be used?
- Are prompts or outputs sensitive?
- Are audit records allowed to include hashes only?

## Success

- What would make the pilot successful?
- What would make the pilot inconclusive?
- What decisions must not be made by the pilot?
- Who must review the final report?
