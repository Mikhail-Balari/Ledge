# Pilot Success Criteria

A pilot can be considered successful when:

- The demo runs reproducibly.
- The unsafe path fails or is blocked in checked execution.
- The confidence threshold is explicit.
- Fallback or human review is explicit.
- Deterministic rules are separated from AI-derived decisions.
- An audit sample exists.
- Audit chain verification is shown.
- A calibration sample exists, or the absence of outcome data is stated as a limitation.
- The client understands where Ledge fits.
- No production-readiness or compliance claim was made.

Failure or inconclusive outcomes are also useful if they reveal unclear
thresholds, missing fallback paths, missing confidence signals, or insufficient
outcome data.
